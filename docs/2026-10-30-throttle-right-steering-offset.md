# Right-hand throttle offset relative to right steering bar

Recorded October 30, 2026, for the autonomous mower throttle linkage CAD instructions. These measurements supplement `docs/2026-10-30-throttle-servo-printable-variants-addendum.md` and apply to all five design variants.

**Mower identification:** 60-inch Toro TimeCutter Max Havoc MyRIDE, **model 77603**, read by user from the mower ID plate on 2026-10-30. Serial number not yet provided.

With throttle fully pulled back (low speed), its observed position is approximately **2.5–3.5 inches (63.5–88.9 mm) to the right of the right steering bar**. With throttle fully forward (full-speed run), its observed position is approximately **3.5–4.5 inches (88.9–114.3 mm) to the right of that same right steering bar**.

The new throttle pushrod must therefore reach a right-offset throttle interface. It must account for changing sideways alignment over the full throttle sweep, without binding or interference with the steering bar, steering pushrod, servo horn, or mount. Maintain independent steering and throttle motion. The continuous, micro-fine sliding pushrod adjustment and fully 3D-printed linkage requirement remain unchanged, as does the 420 × 420 × 500 mm printer envelope and requirement to provide five design alternatives.

The provided ranges are approximate positional measurements, **not confirmed lateral stroke**. Do not assume exactly one inch of sideways movement or replace these ranges with a single fixed offset. Measure and document the throttle lever point, the corresponding right steering bar reference point (surface or centerline), and the direction of measurement at both endpoints. Parameterize unresolved dimensions and verify intermediate clearance and kinematics in CAD. Provide annotated top-view and exploded views for each variant. Preserve positive joint retention and fail-safe throttle return; do not represent the assembly as physically tested until real testing is completed.
