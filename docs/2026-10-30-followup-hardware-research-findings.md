# Lawnberry Pi — continued hardware research and practical findings
Date: 2026-10-30. Research-only update. No purchased hardware was opened, installed, measured or tested.

## P0: 77603 factory PTO/start/ignition facts

1. **Toro 77603 has serial-number-dependent part families**, including 400000000–415399999, 415400000–416999999, 417600000–418960999, 418961000–419002711, 419002712–419999999, 420000000–420789086, 420789087–422099999, and 422100000 onward in the live Toro portal. A single catalog cannot safely stand in for all revisions. [Toro parts portal](https://www.toro.com/en/product/77603); [parts catalog 3476-743A for 418961000 and up](https://www.toro.com/getpub/265916).
2. The **official operator manual** includes schematic G454156 on printed 8-1; the rectangular rocker is the PTO and the round control over ignition is the choke. **Do not assume pin orientation from the small manual drawing**, which is not a connector-face pinout. [Toro official manual](https://www.toro.com/getpub/273204).
3. The parts catalog **lists factory relay 1-643275** as electrical assembly ref. 8, and ignition switch 137-4100, PTO switch 139-6368, solenoid 117-1197, harness 145-6382 for the cited serial range. That **factory relay serves the OEM circuit and is not a spare contact or an already-purchased extra control relay**. [Catalog printed p. 27](https://www.toro.com/getpub/265916#page=27).
4. The official **Kawasaki FR691V service manual ignition-system theory (ELECTRICAL SYSTEM 9-18/9-19)** states that **closing the stop switch routes the ignition primary voltage to ground and prevents ignition**. Thus **engine OFF may require a controlled ground-to-kill interface**, not a series +12 V interruption. This is engine-family behavior, **NOT an identified physical stop lead or tested Toro wiring**; never apply 12 V to an ignition coil kill wire. [Kawasaki service manual](https://www.kawasakipower.com/kengine/3-TechService/Home/Serviceman/99924-2093-06_FR_FS_FX651V_691V_730V_English_eBook_Rev.pdf). The Toro 77603 product lists Kawasaki FR691V 23 hp/726 cc, while **its engine spec suffix and actual installed connections remain unconfirmed**. [Toro specs](https://www.toro.com/en/product/77603).
5. Manufacturer operating interlocks: PTO ON must prevent cranking; drive levers not in PARK must prevent cranking; manual says the engine should stop within one second if operator rises while PTO engaged, or leaves the seat with motion levers unlocked. Retain the OEM circuit and test its behavior as-installed; reported always-occupied seat switch must **not** be credited as a working safety input. [Toro manual page 4-4 to 4-5](https://www.toro.com/getpub/273204#page=24).
6. **Recommended conditional PTO architecture** is still *two independently owned NO, energize-to-allow contacts in series in the OEM-authorized clutch supply*. Pi or Alta can withdraw *its own* permission; **neither** can energize blades when the factory switch forbids it. This is **not** a real connector wiring plan. Existing 2× single-contact HiLetGo modules with **one reserved for steering** leave **only one known PTO contact**. Owned **automotive relay harness has no confirmed relay body**. The Toro factory 1-643275 relay also cannot count as a spare. [Current PTO assessment](2026-10-30-dual-controller-pto-circuit-feasibility.md).
7. **Start and stop are not the same circuit as PTO**. Remote start remains blocked by the mower's OEM PARK interlock until there is a legitimate way to place bars in PARK: the documented fore/aft steering linkage does not swing them outward. Never bypass the interlock. The manufacturer's maximum **5 seconds of cranking and at least 15 seconds recovery after failure** takes precedence over requested 5-second retry interval. [Toro manual](https://www.toro.com/getpub/273204).

## P0: Pololu 2806 purchased steering selector — confirmed behavior

- Purchased **one assembled #2806**, not the partial kit; source selection is by a **valid RC PWM pulse**, not direct two-GPIO shorting or static GPIO SEL.
- Manufacturer guide documents four 2-input mux channels, 2.5–5.5 V VCC for switch logic family, and **NO safe-start for the RC servo multiplexer**. [Pololu guide](https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J60/all).
- Default **SEL ~1700 µs** threshold selects **slave above threshold**; missing/invalid SEL selects **master** when FAILMODE jumper is **OFF**, or holds all outputs low when FAILMODE is **ON**. With Alta originating SEL, manufacturer-default FAILMODE-OFF may return control to healthy Pi on Alta loss. **Pi loss while Alta lives** must be handled by Alta requesting the slave source; only actual device-level tests can verify motion/neutral. If the mux itself is unpowered or damaged, neither source is guaranteed. Do not use FAILMODE-ON and label held-low PWM as servo-neutral. [Pololu product/functional guide](https://www.pololu.com/product/2806) / [manual](https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J60/all).

## P1: Seeed HaLow USB enumeration problem — strong board-specific lead

- **Seeed's official Wio-WM6108_V30 Mini PCIe schematic** uses an FGH100M radio module. The radio is connected using **SPI** signals on the **Mini PCIe connector's repurposed PCM pins 45/47/49/51** plus control lines; Mini PCIe **USB D-/D+ pins 36 and 38 show NO-CONNECT symbols**, and no PCIe lane connection to the FGH100M is shown. So a *passive USB-only Mini PCIe carrier* cannot enumerate this **specific board variant** as a USB device. This is a likely explanation for the owner's prior USB carrier test **IF the owned board matches the schematic**. It does not prove the owner's exact radio/carrier revision yet. [Seeed manufacturer schematic](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wifi_halow/res/Wio-WM6108_V30_SCH_20241107.pdf).
- The Seeed wiki for the WM6108 identifies SPI as its host interface and 3.3 V digital **plus** 5 V RF front-end supplies where needed; **a Mini PCIe form factor does not imply a USB or PCIe data interface**. [Seeed technical guide](https://github.com/Seeed-Studio/wiki-documents/blob/docusaurus-version/sites/en/docs/Network/WiFi-Halow/wifi-halow-module-for-xiao.md).
- **Next:** verify printed SKU/revision and carrier's actual interface; if these match, evaluate a compatible Linux SPI breakout/controller/driver instead of ordering more USB adapters. A 1:1 direct Ethernet link remains the simpler Pi↔Alta supervision channel while HaLow goes to the house/base.

## P1: RTK receiver compatibility — supports low-cost owner-first option

- Official Quectel documents confirm **LC29H (BS)** generates **RTCM base-station corrections**, in fixed-position or survey-in mode. It supports RTCM 3, including MSM4/MSM7 and the stationary reference point **1005** message with documented configuration. [Quectel LC29H hardware manual §1.9.2](https://www.quectel.com/content/uploads/2022/06/Quectel_LC29H_Series_Hardware_Design_V1.2.pdf); [LC29H(BS) protocol manual](https://quectel.com/content/uploads/2024/02/Quectel_LC29HBS_GNSS_Protocol_Specification_V1.0.pdf).
- Quectel identifies the **LC29H (DA)** as an RTK-capable rover (product page specifies **1 Hz RTK**) and BS as the base; compatibility and specific message sets must be confirmed with actual boards/firmware. [Quectel GNSS family overview](https://www.quectel.com/product/gnss-lc29h/).
- **Do not reflexively buy a ZED-F9P** if the earlier planned BS/DA receiver hardware is already owned and delivers the intended navigation update rate and performance. Ownership is **not** confirmed; this is a low-cost conditional alternative, not a statement that any new receiver was purchased. Survey-in gives a local reference, not inherently surveyed absolute centimeter accuracy.

## P1: Four waterproof ultrasonics — compatibility corrections

- Current user target: **four JSN-SR04T**, not three HC-SR04. These are typically 5 V modules with **5 V ECHO that must be level shifted** before any Pi/Alta 3.3 V GPIO; for Raspberry Pi the manufacturer/education guidance stresses this. JSN-family revisions vary and can have 25 cm blind zones depending on version. [JSN-SR04T manual](https://www.openbikesensor.org/docs/other/collective-order/jsn-sr04t-en.pdf); [Raspberry Pi guide](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/see-like-a-bat/0); [JSN vendor guidance](https://shillehtek.com/blogs/shillehtek-product-manuals/jsn-sr04t-waterproof-ultrasonic-distance-sensor-arduino-esp32-manual).
- A legacy wiring table at `docs/ultrasonic-wiring-guide.md` incorrectly assigns **header physical pin 17** as **5V**; physical pin 17 is **3.3 V**. Do **NOT** energize/mount sensors using the outdated table. Four-sensor unique TRIG/ECHO GPIO allocation must be computed against actual Hailo/HaLow/I2C/relay/camera pin budget; mark pending rather than give conflicting fixed pins. Never assert ultrasonics alone can prove absence of people or animals.
